Underwater Exoskeleton for Diver Assistance

FSU Undergraduate Research Opportunity Program · September 2025 – June 2026 · Advised by Dr. Higgins

Objective: Design a waterproof, wrist-mounted input device a diver can use to control an assist exoskeleton, where drag and current already limit mobility.

Result: A wrist-mounted controller modeled in SolidWorks, integrating four input buttons and a force-sensitive resistor with routed internal wire channels, 3D-printed as a waterproof housing for testing. The FSR registers 2–3 N of applied force per press, enough to transmit reliable control signals underwater. Prototype is built; in-water testing is the next phase.

Tools: SolidWorks, 3D printing, force-sensitive resistor, sensors & instrumentation, waterproof sealing
